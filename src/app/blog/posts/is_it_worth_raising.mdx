Raising with VC seems like a kiss of death. I recently stumbled upon [this study ](https://dealroom.co/news/165269-88-of-vc-backed-founders-get-nothing-at-exit-and-the-top-1-take-79-of-th/)by the university of Zurich and the Wharton School of Business and its conclusions are pretty daming. 

Close to 90% of funders who sucessfully exit actuallt get out with excatly… zero cash. We're talking about the very small minority of founders who actually exits successfully. This is a version of capitalism that is not only predatory but also deeply discouraging. This is a version of capitalism that only proliferates because of the myth of the successful founder. 

The reality is far dimmer. VC-backed founders spent countless hours, days, months, building a product, only to see its fruit being pulled from their hands. This is just plain exploitation, in a way that Marx described it. 

There is a lot to be said about the founder's myth, and why it still pulls so much weight. One thing for sure: the system works only if people believe in it.
